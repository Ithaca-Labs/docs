openx402 is a permissively licensed, self-hostable x402 v2 facilitator for
Stellar. It verifies and settles paid HTTP or MCP resources, sponsors network
fees without custodying seller funds, catalogs Bazaar metadata, and exposes
discovery tools for agents.

The shipped `exact` flow is live on Stellar testnet. The Soroban-backed
`upto` flow is proposed and has testnet evidence. Pubnet is packaged but gated
until its release requirements are complete.
